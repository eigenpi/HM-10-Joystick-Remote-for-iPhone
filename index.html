Command byte 0b00000000 0x00 STOP

Release your finger to center the joystick and request STOP (0x00). Commands are sent every 100 ms while connected.

20%

Small movements inside the dead zone are treated as centered. Diagonal commands combine two bits. 


   

   

    Safety: The receiver must independently stop motors if no valid command arrives within approximately 300–500 ms. Browser disconnects and phone sleep can prevent STOP from reaching the receiver.
